Questions, answered

Do I need an account to upload?

Yes — uploads are tied to a free account so your files, links and download counts stay under your control. Downloading never requires an account.

How large can a file be?

It depends on your plan. The Free plan allows files up to 100 MB and 1,024 MB total storage; paid plans raise limits up to 500 MB per file and 10,240 MB storage.
